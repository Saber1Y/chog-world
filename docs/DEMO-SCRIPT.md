# CHOG World: video scripts

Two videos:

1. **Motion intro (35 seconds):** an animated brand film, rendered from code in `video/`.
   It opens the demo and works on its own for the X launch post.
2. **Product demo (3 minutes):** a screen recording on testnet, with the motion intro as the first 10 seconds.

Voice: VoiceStudio's **The Neighbor** (young adult American woman, conversational), generated locally.
Every line is a separate file in `video/voiceover/`, so it can be re-timed without re-recording.

Links to show:

- In the X post: the mainnet pilot, https://chog-world-mainnet.vercel.app
- In the demo: recorded on testnet, https://chog-world-zeta.vercel.app, because no Chog Genesis holder has played yet.
  Say so on screen once, honestly.

## 1. Motion intro (35 seconds)

Style: the site's sticker look.
Night-purple ground, thick ink outlines, chunky offset shadows, Monad purple and coin gold as the loud colours, Lilita One for headlines.
Motion is springy but short: things pop in from 90% scale, never from zero, and nothing lingers.

| Time | Picture | Voiceover (The Neighbor) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:04 | A single Chog card pops onto an empty night background. | "This is a Chog." |
| 0:04-0:08 | The card sits still. A grey "in a wallet" tag; a little dust drifts by. | "Right now, it just sits in your wallet." |
| 0:08-0:13 | A wallet badge and a spark snap onto the card; the frame lights up and a LV 1 badge appears. | "CHOG World gives it a wallet and a mind of its own." |
| 0:13-0:22 | Fast montage of three stickers: a quest card ("Slime Pit +20 XP"), an arena VS card, and a journal thought typing itself out. | "It quests, picks fights with rivals and earns, all on its own, inside the limits you set." |
| 0:22-0:28 | The level counter climbs from 1 to 12, the frame upgrades from plain to bronze to gold, and title ribbons stack up. | "Every win, every scar, every title becomes part of it." |
| 0:28-0:32 | The card slides from one wallet to another, carrying its loot with it. | "And everything it earns moves with the NFT." |
| 0:32-0:35 | Logo, then the tagline, then the URL. | "CHOG World. Every Chog has a look. Now give it a life." |

## 2. Product demo (3 minutes, testnet)

Before recording:

- Use a wallet with test MON on Monad testnet, and keep a second, already-living Chog ready (for example Chog #1) to show the journal, the arena and the leaderboard without waiting.
- Record in Screen Studio at 1440x900, with the browser zoomed to 100% and the dev "N" badge not visible (use the live site, not localhost).
- Cut every wait for a transaction; keep the moment each one confirms.

| Time | Screen | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:10 | The motion intro, cut to its first 10 seconds. | (from the intro) |
| 0:10-0:30 | **Landing page.** Slow scroll: the hero, the top Chogs, "How it works", the live world feed. | "This is CHOG World. Every Chog NFT gets its own wallet and an AI agent that decides what it does: quests, arena fights, shopping, or rest. Everything happens onchain, and the owner sets the limits." |
| 0:30-0:45 | **My Chogs.** Connect the wallet and press *Mint a free test Chog*. | "I'm on testnet, so I can mint a free test Chog. On mainnet it's your real Chog Genesis." |
| 0:45-1:25 | **Awaken wizard.** Step 1, give it a wallet (free). Step 2, *Let it act* (one signature). Step 3, type a name and a personality, pick goals and the Balanced budget, and sign. Step 4, get test CHOG, send 100, and add 0.5 MON for gas. | "Awakening takes four steps. It gets its own wallet. I let the game's rules contract act for it, never our server.
